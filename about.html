BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A few good
questions.

Can you improve the website I already have?

Yes. Existing-site improvement is the default. Start with your URL, what should change and what you need to keep. We confirm the content and integrations to preserve before production.

What do I get before paying?

An editable first-look concept shaped by the details you enter. It gives you a starting point to explore. Research, three complete directions and the full website are part of a paid project.

How long does a project take?

Page count, content and integrations determine the schedule. We confirm it with your exact scope before payment. Your project space shows actual progress.

Will I own the finished website?

You receive the approved source, created assets and handoff notes. Third-party services and licensed materials retain their own terms, which we identify in the scope.
