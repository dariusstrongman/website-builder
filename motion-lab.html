Premium motion is art direction.

Continuity

Each scene inherits a subject, rhythm, or spatial idea from the last instead of starting from zero.

Purpose

Motion clarifies the product or story. If stillness communicates better, stillness wins.

Device craft

Desktop, phone, and reduced-motion states are intentionally composed and reviewed as separate experiences.

This is an experimental visual proof for the new Website Builder motion contract. It is not a claim that every customer site should look or move like this.
