Before you decide

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Why pay for this instead of prompting a chatbot?

The intended service is the work around the prompt: buyer research, distinct creative options, a coherent multi-page design, responsive implementation and review. Inspect our working studies and the detailed comparison to decide whether that is valuable for your business.

What counts as a revision round?

One consolidated set of feedback on the agreed design or implementation. A new direction after approval, additional pages or new features changes the scope and must be agreed separately.

Will my website rank on Google?

Search foundations help make a website understandable and discoverable. They do not guarantee rankings, traffic or sales. Competition, useful content, business reputation and ongoing work matter too. See what search-ready design includes.

Do I get the website and its source?

The proposed handoff includes approved source, created assets and setup notes. Third-party fonts, media and services retain their own licenses. Any necessary licenses or subscriptions should be identified in the scope.

Can I buy a package today?

Not yet. This site demonstrates the proposed experience and pricing. You can explore the sample brief and directions, but there is no active checkout or commissioned build behind the prototype.
