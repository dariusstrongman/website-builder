# Website Builder: benchmark-to-build standard

This is a project operating standard, not a claim that the site has earned a score.
Updated 2026-09-04. Do not change prices or invent commercial proof to improve the presentation.

## What was actually inspected

- User-supplied Gamma homepage screenshots: desktop Products and Solutions menus, hero typography, product imagery, section transition. These are not pricing-page screenshots.
- Gamma pricing public content: plan fit descriptions, aligned offer hierarchy, progressive feature lists, FAQ. Live visual inspection was blocked by a repeated verification screen. Do not claim its exact spacing or pricing interaction was inspected.
- Clay live desktop homepage: sculptural brand scene, persistent compact navigation, split headline/action group, contained proof collection immediately after hero.
- Tend live desktop homepage: an anxiety-reducing message, portrait participating in the composition, direct booking action.
- Sol Gold Benchmark Library v2 supplied by Darius: confirmed preference anchors and industry routes. Proxy ratings are not user-confirmed ratings.

## Transfer the reason, not the decoration

| Observed strength | Why it works | Our implementation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gamma's consistent navy, white and blue interface | The visual art can vary without fragmenting the brand | One ink/white/indigo interface system; imagery supplies secondary color |
| Gamma's named navigation groups | Depth becomes discoverable without a row of 12 links | Website and Resources disclosure menus, with descriptive links and a mobile layout |
| Gamma's product imagery paired with concrete explanations | Visitors see the benefit rather than reading abstract promises | Clickable, industry-specific website specimens and a visible sample workflow |
| Gamma's plan-fit descriptions and feature hierarchy | Customers can compare scope quickly | Equal-format $500/$1,000/$1,500 offers, one-time billing, matched rows, clear exclusions |
| Clay's distinctive hero followed by proof | Personality is grounded by evidence | Curated specimen gallery plus honest design-study labels, never borrowed customer logos |
| Tend's low-anxiety proposition and direct action | The page answers the buyer's emotional objection | Show a direction before commitment; explain what the customer controls |

## Non-negotiable decisions

1. Buyer: a small-business owner who wants a credible, distinctive website without managing a design project. This is a marketing-site prototype, not production generation or checkout.
2. Interface palette: ink, white, cool neutral, one indigo action color. No random green. No full-section rainbow switching. Secondary color belongs to images or the example's own brand.
3. Type: a consistent legible functional family, display hierarchy scaled by viewport, natural letter spacing. Body 16px minimum. Regular controls 14px minimum. No giant headings that hide useful work.
4. Imagery: one authored brand asset; product specimens must look like websites people could actually buy. No decorative metric graphs, fake dashboards or invented social proof.
5. Rhythm: hero to visual evidence to mechanism to scope. Alternate composition, not arbitrary color. Every page must have its own useful job.
6. Range: architecture, wellness and industrial technology must differ in layout, type, image treatment and conversion story. Relabeling identical layouts does not prove range.
7. Motion: use it to show selection, state or hierarchy. Native scrolling. Reduced-motion support. No whole-page invisible reveal defaults.
8. Truth: a fictional study demonstrates craft, not customer outcomes. Future capabilities and intended prices must remain labeled as prototype scope.
9. SEO: crawlable useful content, honest metadata, navigation and internal links. More pages alone are not a ranking strategy. Private hosting is not a public organic-acquisition launch.
10. QA: verify links, fragments, assets, headings, metadata, form labels, dropdown keyboard behavior, device toggles, 390/768/1440 layouts when preview is available. Do not equate source checks with visual testing.

## Before the next release

- Compare the actual rendered pages, not just their code, whenever the environment permits it.
- Recheck the entire customer path after a shared CSS or navigation change.
- Keep meaningful proof close to buying decisions.
- State remaining limitations without claiming a guaranteed 10/10 or conversion lift.
