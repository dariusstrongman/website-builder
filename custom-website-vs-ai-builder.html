Comparison / Build method

AI can generate a page. The hard part is deciding what deserves to ship.

A one-shot prompt is fast and often useful. Managed production adds research, creative choice, responsive judgment, verification and accountability.

7 minute guideUpdated September 2026AI-assisted, human-directed
IN THIS GUIDESide-by-sideChoose a prompt whenChoose managed whenOur difference

The honest comparison

DecisionOne AI promptManaged custom build
Starting pointYour instructionsBuyer, offer and competitor research
Creative choiceUsually one generated directionThree structurally different routes
ResponsibilityYou inspect and repairThe production process owns completion
MobileOften responsive by defaultComposed and checked as its own experience
TruthYou verify every claimUnsupported claims are qualified or removed
DeliveryGenerated code or hosted pageFinished scope, revisions and source handoff

Use a DIY prompt when

You need a temporary page, enjoy directing design, can evaluate code and accessibility, and the cost of getting it wrong is low. For an internal experiment or weekend idea, paying for managed production may be unnecessary.

Use managed production when

The website represents a real business, buyers will judge credibility through it, multiple pages must work together, or you do not want to become the designer, copywriter, developer and quality reviewer.

What we are actually selling

Not access to a model. The product is a visible decision system: a focused brief, route-specific research, three real creative directions, a complete build after approval, and a release standard that blocks serious failures.

AI accelerates parts of the work. It does not remove the need for judgment.
